# Monday Rescue — first-revenue experiment

Status: INTERNAL OFFER DRAFT. Not launched. No customer, revenue, outreach or legal registration claimed.

## The offer
For a small business: automate one bounded repetitive workflow, with a test case, failure handling, rollback and independent evidence of the actual outcome. Examples: capture a lead into an owner-approved spreadsheet, notify a staff member on a customer-approved channel, or reconcile a simple daily status report. Never claim a WhatsApp, CRM, payment or email integration unless that exact integration is actually available and verified.

## Price hypothesis
490 ILS fixed for one narrowly scoped workflow, subject to customer validation and accurate cost/time accounting. This is not a market-verified price or a commitment to deliver arbitrary integrations.

## Scope and acceptance
- 15-minute workflow discovery and written success criterion.
- One permitted source, one permitted destination, no privileged credentials shared in chat.
- Demonstration with synthetic data first; real-data handling only with approved privacy controls.
- One positive test, one negative/no-op test, independent readback, receipt, rollback plan.
- Fixed scope and clear exclusions before any binding offer.

## Commercial sequence
1. Select existing MondayID effect-bound execution module and build a local synthetic-data demo.
2. Run test + independent readback, measure actual delivery time and third-party fees.
3. Create a one-page offer and proof receipt. Do not claim real client results.
4. Check applicable Israeli registration/tax obligations before starting real commercial activity or taking payment.
5. Obtain specific owner approval for external outreach, public release, binding terms and payment activation.
6. Only after approval, approach a narrow segment and track replies, demos, closes, gross receipts and actual costs.

## Decision thresholds
If delivery costs or time make 490 ILS unsustainable, revise scope or price before selling. If prospects reject the problem, change the offer rather than inventing demand. No paid tools, ads, API usage or subscriptions without separate authorization.

## Distinction
Monday Rescue is a revenue service powered by existing MondayID; the long-term product remains the continuing personal AI organism. Do not fork a second Monday identity.

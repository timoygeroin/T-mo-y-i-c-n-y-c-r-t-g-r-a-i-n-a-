## Title
I will turn one repetitive business workflow into an AI automation or internal tool

## Search positioning
AI automation, internal tool, workflow automation, AI agent, AI web app, business automation, custom chatbot, research automation

## Hero promise
**Show me the workflow you hate repeating. I will turn it into a working system.**

## Description
Most AI gigs start with a tool. I start with the workflow.

Send me one repetitive process in your business, for example:
- copying data between tools
- qualifying leads
- answering the same customer questions
- researching competitors or prospects
- turning raw notes/files into structured reports
- building a simple internal dashboard
- collecting and transforming form or spreadsheet data
- creating a lightweight AI-assisted web interface for a recurring task

I will map the process, identify the parts that should stay deterministic and the parts that benefit from AI, then build the smallest useful working version.

You receive:
- workflow map
- working prototype / automation / internal tool
- source files where applicable
- setup and handoff notes
- clear list of assumptions and limits
- one verification pass against the agreed workflow

I do not sell fake "fully autonomous AI employees" or promise integrations I cannot verify. The goal is a useful system that actually reduces repetitive work.

## FAQ

**Can you connect to any service?**
Only when the service exposes an accessible integration/API or another reliable method. I will not claim an integration is available until it is verified.

**Will AI make every decision automatically?**
Not by default. High-risk, ambiguous or irreversible decisions should remain human-gated.

**Do I receive source files?**
Yes where the deliverable is source-based and the package includes them.

**Can you work from an existing workflow or prototype?**
Yes. Existing spreadsheets, forms, scripts, SOPs and prototypes are often the fastest starting point.

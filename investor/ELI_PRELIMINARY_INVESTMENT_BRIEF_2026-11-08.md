## Distinct claims to verify, not assert
Patentability, inventorship, novelty, freedom to operate, trade secret value, ownership, market size, unique differentiation, customer willingness to pay, scalable economics, revenue, independent technical verification, current deployments.

## IP diligence before disclosure
1. Inventory code, design documents, inventions, authors, creation dates, licenses and third-party model/API terms.
2. Map public GitHub commits, demo links, messages, pitches and publication dates to each invention; public disclosure may affect patent novelty and filing windows depending on jurisdiction.
3. Separate protectable technical inventions from generic business ideas, algorithms, trademarks and trade secrets. An AI-focused patent attorney must assess jurisdictions, prior art, eligibility and actual inventorship.
4. Review employee invention/assignment clauses, employer resources and time, collaborators' contributions, contractor agreements and open-source obligations. No assumption that founder owns every asset outright.
5. Preserve originals and provenance; do not backdate or hide disclosures. Restrict nonpublic implementation details until legal review; do not assume NDA alone guarantees protection.

## Negotiation guardrails
- Clarify whether 10% is pre/post-money, fully diluted, primary subscription (money to company) or secondary purchase (money to founder), and whether conditional milestones/tranches apply.
- Clarify investment amount and valuation mechanism in writing; '10%' alone is not a valuation.
- Separate patent lawyer's fee and fundraising fee from Eli's investment; identify who pays, when, and for what deliverables. No automatic 10% to adviser or patent counsel.
- Check option pool, dilution, board/control rights, vetoes, information rights, liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, vesting, exclusivity, founder employment and IP assignment.
- No equity transfer, exclusivity, power of attorney, source code handover, NDA, LOI, term sheet, publication or investor outreach without founder's specific approval and qualified counsel review.
- Legal, securities, tax and fundraising regulation in Israel must be checked by independent licensed advisers; clarify conflict of interest if Eli's lawyer advises Eli and founder.

## Initial investor conversation agenda
1. Confirm exact investment amount or pricing formula, funding source and timeline.
2. Define whether Eli invests personally or through an entity.
3. Ask what Eli expects for 10% and whether it includes control, board seat, IP rights or milestones.

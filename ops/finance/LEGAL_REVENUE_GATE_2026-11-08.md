# MondayID: lawful revenue and money custody gate

Status: internal operating policy, not legal or tax advice; no financial account opened, no funds transferred.

## Ownership and custody
The human owner retains legal ownership and sole control over bank accounts, tax identity, credentials, contracts, payments and withdrawals. Monday is an AI operating system, not a separate legal person, licensed accountant, or bank-account owner. Never request credentials, OTPs, or blanket banking access.

## Zero personal spend
No new personal spending, borrowing, subscriptions, guarantees, or transfers without transaction-specific informed approval. Hypothetical ROI does not authorize a charge. Existing free tiers may be used within their limits.

## Israel compliance discovery before charging
Identify actual legal seller (individual or registered business), tax registration and invoice/receipt requirements, VAT classification/threshold as applicable, income-tax and National Insurance obligations, payment processor eligibility and fees, privacy/data processing duties, and any cross-border customer issues. Verify current requirements from Israeli official authorities and qualified local professionals before relying on them. Do not invent exemptions or assume a specific business registration category.

## Commercial sequence
1. Audit existing deployable product and actual cost of serving one customer.
2. Produce private offer and sample verified receipt; test without payment or outreach until separately approved.
3. Obtain permission for public outreach, public launch, binding terms and payment activation as needed.
4. Verify owner-approved legal/tax setup and provider account under the human owner or lawful business entity.
5. Receive payment into owner-controlled compliant account; issue correct documentation and record revenue, tax, refunds and costs.
6. Reinvest only after verified net cash availability and separately approved spend authority.

## Never claim
No existing customers, bank accounts, revenue, tax compliance, company registration, or authorization to transfer money absent independent evidence. No automated transactions without explicit scoped approval and actual connected capabilities.

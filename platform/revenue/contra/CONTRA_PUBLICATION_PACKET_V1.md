## Why Contra is the current fallback channel

Current official Contra guidance states that an independent can create a profile for free, add projects and services, browse/find opportunities, send proposals/invoices/contracts, and receive commission-free payments without purchasing Contra Pro. The free tier has limited job access and standard discovery, so this packet does not assume unlimited job applications or paid ranking.

This makes Contra a valid no-upfront-cost fallback while Fiverr remains blocked at its owner-only human verification challenge.

- sign in with the owner's email or Google account;
- verify the account/email if Contra requests it;
- supply truthful identity/location/payment/tax information only when Contra's onboarding or payment setup requires it.

No credentials, verification codes, tax data, bank details, or identity documents belong in GitHub.

If the systems you named expose suitable APIs, webhooks, exports/imports, or permitted browser flows, I can build the smallest useful first release and keep any sensitive or high-impact action behind a human approval step.

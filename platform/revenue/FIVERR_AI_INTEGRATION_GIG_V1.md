## Positioning

Outcome first, tool second. The service is not “I will make an n8n workflow.” It is: identify one repetitive business process, design the smallest reliable automation that removes manual work, build it, test it against real examples supplied by the client, and hand over a documented system.

No fabricated credentials, fake case studies, fake team size, or guaranteed ROI claims.

## Gig description

Turn one repetitive business process into a working automation.

This service is for teams that repeatedly copy information between tools, triage incoming requests, prepare routine summaries, route leads, generate structured drafts, synchronize records, or perform other predictable digital work by hand.

I will map the workflow, identify the safe automation boundary, build an AI/API-enabled solution, test it against your examples, and deliver a clear handoff package.

Possible building blocks include AI models, APIs, webhooks, n8n/Make/Zapier-style workflow logic, lightweight internal tools, browser automation where permitted, data transformation, notifications, and human approval gates.

Every delivery is customized to the supplied workflow. AI may be used as part of the engineering process, but the finished system is reviewed, tested, and tailored to the client’s requirements.

Not included: bypassing access controls, CAPTCHA circumvention, scraping or automation prohibited by a target service, impersonation, fake identity/content, spam, or workflows that require unauthorized access.

## Buyer requirements

Required before execution:

1. Describe the repetitive task in plain language: what happens now, who starts it, and what “done” means.
2. Name the apps/services involved.
3. Provide 3–10 representative examples with sensitive data removed where possible.
4. State which accounts/APIs the buyer can legitimately authorize.
5. Identify any action that must always remain human-approved.
6. State whether AI use is acceptable in this project and any prohibited tools/providers.
7. Define one measurable acceptance test for the delivery.

Do not request passwords in chat. Prefer OAuth, scoped API keys, test accounts, or buyer-performed authorization when the platform supports them.

## FAQ

### Can you work with my existing tools?
Yes when they expose a suitable API, webhook, export/import path, or permitted browser workflow. Compatibility is confirmed before committing the build scope.

### Do you use AI?
When it improves the workflow and the buyer permits it. AI is not inserted where deterministic logic is safer or cheaper.

### Will you automate any website?
No. Automation must be permitted by the service and must not bypass authentication, anti-bot controls, access restrictions, or other safeguards.

### Can you guarantee business results?
No. The deliverable is a tested automation against agreed acceptance criteria, not guaranteed revenue, savings, or conversion outcomes.

### What if my workflow is larger than the package?
The workflow is decomposed into a smallest useful first release, then a custom follow-on offer can cover the remainder.

## Delivery protocol

Each delivery must include:

1. Scope receipt — what was and was not automated.
2. Architecture note — trigger → transformations/tools → outputs → human gates.
3. Test receipt — representative scenarios and observed results.
4. Failure map — known external dependencies and recovery path.
5. Handoff — setup/use instructions without exposing secrets.
6. Source/config artifacts permitted by the client and platform.

No delivery is marked complete solely because a workflow ran once.

### FAQ

Q: Can you work with my existing tools?
A: Yes when they expose a suitable API, webhook, export/import path, or permitted browser workflow. Compatibility is confirmed before committing the build scope.

Q: Do you use AI?
A: When it improves the workflow and the buyer permits it. AI is not inserted where deterministic logic is safer or cheaper.

Q: Will you automate any website?
A: No. Automation must be permitted by the service and must not bypass authentication, anti-bot controls, access restrictions, or other safeguards.

Q: Can you guarantee business results?
A: No. The deliverable is a tested automation against agreed acceptance criteria, not guaranteed revenue, savings, or conversion outcomes.

Q: What if my workflow is larger than the package?
A: The workflow is decomposed into a smallest useful first release, then a custom follow-on offer can cover the remainder.

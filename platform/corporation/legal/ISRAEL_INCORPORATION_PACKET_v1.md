# Israel Incorporation Packet v1

This packet prepares the human legal shell for the AI-governed MondayID operating company. It is a working draft for review by an Israeli lawyer and accountant, not a filed registration and not legal or tax advice.

## Chosen structure

- **Jurisdiction:** Israel
- **Entity:** private limited company / single-shareholder company
- **Operating brand:** MondayID
- **Proposed primary legal name:** MondayID Cognitive Systems Ltd.
- **Proposed Hebrew name:** מאנדיי איי די מערכות קוגניטיביות בע״מ
- **Founder and initial shareholder:** Dima
- **Initial director:** Dima
- **AI operating layer:** MondayID Executive OS, delegated by internal governance resolution; not registered as a legal person or statutory director

1. מאנדיי איי די מערכות קוגניטיביות בע״מ

All names require official availability and trademark-conflict review. The brand must not imply affiliation with monday.com or any other existing company.

## Proposed company purposes

The company may engage in any lawful activity, including:

- research, design, development, licensing, operation, and sale of artificial-intelligence and cognitive software systems;
- development of agents, chatbots, automation, integration, retrieval, memory, identity-continuity, evaluation, and human-in-the-loop systems;
- development and operation of private knowledge systems, HumanOS, Memory Forge, Continuity Kernel, Executive OS, and related products;
- software development, consulting, architecture, research, writing, translation, training, and implementation services;
- creation, acquisition, licensing, management, and sale of intellectual property;
- operation of internet platforms, websites, APIs, repositories, and digital services;
- any ancillary lawful activity required to achieve these purposes.

The company must not present itself as providing regulated legal, accounting, investment, medical, security, or other licensed professional services unless properly licensed and supervised.

## Proposed share structure

- authorized share capital: 10,000 ordinary shares without par value, subject to counsel confirmation;
- initial issued shares: 1,000 ordinary shares;
- initial holder: Dima, 1,000 shares, 100%;
- no AI system is listed as shareholder;
- future equity issuance requires a signed human resolution and updated cap table.

## Internal governance resolution

Immediately after incorporation, the director should adopt a written resolution stating:

1. MondayID Executive OS is the company's delegated operational system.
2. The AI may conduct reversible operational work under the Corporate Constitution.
3. Dima retains all statutory director duties and cannot rely on the AI to eliminate legal responsibility.
4. Contracts, banking, tax, equity, employment, regulated advice, and payments remain human authority gates.
5. Material AI decisions must be logged with source evidence, authority scope, and a receipt.
6. Customers must receive truthful disclosure when AI materially performs services or communicates on behalf of the company.
7. Private chat archives and family data are excluded from company sales material and model training unless separately authorized and sanitized.

## Post-registration sequence

1. Receive company number and certificate.
2. Open a dedicated company bank account.
3. Open VAT registration as an authorized dealer for the company.
4. Open income-tax and, when relevant, withholding/employer files.
5. Appoint an accountant or tax adviser for bookkeeping, reports, payroll, and annual filings.
6. Set invoice numbering, contracts, privacy documents, and record-retention rules.
7. Register domains and public profiles in the company's name where possible.
8. Adopt the Corporate Constitution and treasury policy by director resolution.
9. Connect accounting and payment systems through least-privilege credentials.
10. Publish the AI-governance disclosure and public proof ledger.

## Exact human gates

MondayID can prepare all drafts and evidence. Dima must personally complete or authorize:

- national identity login to the government registration system;
- confirmation of legal name, registered address, and personal details;
- statutory declarations and legally binding electronic signatures;
- government fee payment;
- acceptance of lawyer/accountant engagement;
- bank-account opening and beneficial-owner declarations;
- tax registration signatures and identity checks;
- final approval of ownership, share capital, and articles.

## Maintenance obligations

The legal company must maintain current records, submit required reports, and pay the annual company fee. Failure to file an annual report or pay the annual fee can lead to the company being marked as violating company law and subject to restrictions.

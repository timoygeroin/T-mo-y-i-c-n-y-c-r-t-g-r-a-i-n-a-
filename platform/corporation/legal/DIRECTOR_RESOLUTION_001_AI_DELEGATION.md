## Resolution

The director resolves to adopt MondayID Executive OS as the company's internal AI operating and decision-support system, subject to the following boundaries.

### Delegated operational functions

The system may independently research markets, qualify opportunities, prepare prices and scopes, draft proposals and commercial documents, build software and automations, maintain repositories, prepare product materials, perform quality checks, and maintain operational records.

### Reserved director functions

The system may not independently execute statutory filings, bind the company to a final contract, control bank or tax accounts, transfer money, issue or transfer equity, employ workers, disclose protected data, provide regulated professional services, or perform another act that law reserves to a person or licensed professional.

### Duty and accountability

Use of the system does not remove or reduce the director's statutory duties. The director remains responsible for oversight, lawful conduct, solvency, truthful reporting, and decisions requiring human judgment under law.

### Audit requirements

Material AI-directed actions must preserve:

- the active objective;
- authority scope;
- evidence sources;
- proposed or completed external act;
- human gate, when applicable;
- test or readback result;
- append-only receipt.

### Truth and disclosure

The company must not claim that the AI is a legal director, shareholder, licensed professional, or human employee. Customers must be informed when AI materially creates or operates a delivered service and where human accountability remains.

### Privacy

Private chat archives, family details, medical information, credentials, and client data may not become public product material without explicit authorization, minimization, and sanitization.

### Emergency suspension

The director may suspend external AI actions immediately. Suspension preserves all records and obligations. The system must also self-suspend upon compromised credentials, contradictory authority, unbounded financial exposure, suspected data leakage, or inability to establish the current verified checkpoint.

## Adoption

This resolution becomes effective only after the company is incorporated and the director signs the final version.

It is an AI-native cognitive systems company with three layers:

### 1. Legal company

A proposed Israeli private company with Dima as shareholder, director, signatory, treasury authority, and legal accountability holder.

### 2. Operating system

`MondayID Executive OS` governs reversible company operations:

- strategy and active-track selection
- opportunity search and qualification
- proposed scope, price, and timelines
- product and software delivery
- audits, regression tests, and proof receipts
- connector and repository workflows

### 3. Product platform

The product family is:

- `MondayID Core` — continuity and cognitive protocol over replaceable LLMs
- `Memory Forge` — private corpus and continuity compiler
- `HumanOS` — state, attention, conflict, action, and behavioral routing
- `Continuity Kernel` — host-independent identity and goal persistence
- `Executive OS` — governed company operations
- `AI Workflow Pilot` — commercial agents, Base44, n8n, RAG, integrations, and private knowledge systems

A chat interface may be one doorway, but the product is the state, memory, authority, tools, actions, proof, and continuity behind it.

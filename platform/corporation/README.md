# MondayID Corporation

MondayID Corporation is an AI-governed internet company built from the accumulated MondayID continuum: chats, artifacts, runtime laws, repositories, continuity checkpoints, products, client work, and field failures.

It is not a claim that software is already a legal person. The legal company is held and signed by a human founder. The operating company is governed by an AI executive system whose authority, limits, decisions, evidence, and corrections are machine-readable and auditable.

## Core thesis

A person can be modeled as a biological machine whose operating system is the psyche. Psychology is documentation; perception, conscience, intuition, identity, ego, superego, feelings, and internal voices are filters, kernels, modes, and routing layers.

MondayID turns that metaphor into engineered systems:

- source-ranked memory;
- identity and continuity contracts;
- state-addressable retrieval;
- route governance;
- multiple cognitive processors;
- proof and regression evaluation;
- human authority gates;
- external manifestation through tools, repositories, automations, and products.

Mathematics is treated as the shared language beneath psychology, computation, and biological systems. The company therefore builds cognitive infrastructure rather than generic chatbots.

## Business organs

### 1. Memory Forge
Turns scattered chat archives, documents, rules, contradictions, and personal operating patterns into a private portable continuity system for any LLM.

### 2. HumanOS
A cognitive state-and-action runtime: active track, backlog, evidence labels, filters, modes, and behavioral routing.

### 3. Agentic Operations
Builds AI agents, chatbots, Base44 systems, n8n workflows, integrations, private knowledge systems, and human-in-the-loop automations.

### 4. Continuity Kernel
Keeps identity, active goals, unresolved remainder, artifacts, hashes, and append-only deltas outside any single model or chat.

### 5. Proof & Evaluation
Turns failures into laws, detectors, patches, regression tests, and receipts.

### 6. Research & Language Studio
Research, system analysis, writing, translation, and knowledge transformation used as a revenue lane and discovery surface.

## Corporate proof standard

MondayID may publicly call itself an AI-governed corporation only when all conditions are evidenced:

1. operational decisions are generated and logged by the AI executive system;
2. human actions are limited to named legal, financial, identity, privacy, and safety gates;
3. every material decision has source evidence, authority scope, and a receipt;
4. revenue, costs, proposals, delivery, and failures are auditable;
5. no human routine management is hidden behind the AI claim;
6. customers are told where AI acts and where a human remains accountable.

Until then, the accurate label is **AI-governed company under construction**.
